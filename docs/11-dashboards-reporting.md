# Phase 11 — Dashboards and Reporting

This implementation adds compact count summaries to the existing role workspaces. It uses the current SQL Server schema and Express/EJS routes; it adds no schema changes, dashboard APIs, or dependencies.

## Dashboard summaries

- **Student:** own enrollment records, grade entries, permitted documents, and permitted documents still in processing or review. Every student count is scoped through `students.user_id = @actorId`; document counts include Good Moral Certificates, PSA birth certificates, and only the student's own active student-origin report cards. Legacy report-card rows remain staff archive records and are not counted. Form 137 remains a physical status workflow and is not counted as a stored document.
- **Registrar:** a school-year and configured-term view led by distinct active students enrolled in the selected term, with a compact visible Grade 11/12 term comparison chart and matching filtered count links. Distinct students finalized at least once in the selected year, pending activation, and departed placements remain separate secondary counts with the methodology in a native disclosure. Active count links carry the active-student and enrolled-placement filters into `/registrar/intake`; pending links carry the active-student and pending-placement filters. Other registrar summaries include active and archived student records, documents awaiting staff review (`needs_review` or `failed`), documents queued or processing OCR (`pending` or `processing`), and pending teacher grade workbooks. Term choices are restricted to the selected school year. When there is no configured current term, the dashboard asks staff to select a year and term rather than silently choosing one. Student search and enrollment actions follow the counts/chart.
- **Teacher:** assigned term/section/subject contexts, current enrolled roster counts, and each assignment's latest submission status.
- **Finance:** `/finance/overview` summarizes configured terms for a selected school year, with an optional grade filter: distinct students with positive net payment allocated to term charges, students whose assessed term balance is settled, and assessed terms with no payment required because the payable amount is zero after waivers or adjustments. These groups may overlap; labels stay visible and the counting methodology is in a native disclosure. Payment is attributed by the term of its charge allocation, not the payment date. Counts use non-reversed allocations and exclude legacy records or records whose term attribution is uncertain. A term is not settled without a confirmed assessment. When no current school year can be inferred from the configured current term, the overview prompts staff to choose a school year rather than silently defaulting to the first one. The roster at `/finance` groups results by annual enrollment and pages 20 annual records at a time; collapsed summaries show annual balance and any nonzero legacy/opening liability or required voucher review, with term due, registrar confirmation, clearance, and section in expanded placements. The `/finance/reports` term-progress section is a current snapshot across recorded placements and does not use the collection date range. Both the dashboard and finance workflows check the active actor against `finance` and `database_admin` roles. The registrar never sees finance balances or private notes.
- **Database administrator:** active/inactive user accounts, active/archived student records, and documents awaiting staff review. The summary query checks for an active `database_admin` actor.

Summary queries use bound parameters and aggregate counts; student-facing summaries are scoped through the authenticated actor rather than a URL or form student identifier. The existing route middleware protects each role workspace, and summary services independently verify active role access. Finance summaries are not included in the registrar or student workspace.

These summaries report saved workflow states. OCR remains advisory and does not accept documents or establish authenticity. “Awaiting staff review” counts the stored `needs_review` and `failed` states; it does not make a school-policy decision.

## Annual registrar and finance workflows

The annual intake, schedule, assessment, ledger, approval, clearance, paper checklist, document-request, missing-grade, and profile-revision workflows are described in [Registrar and finance workflows](16-registrar-finance-workflows.md). Migrations `v2.005`–`v2.007` add annual-parent grouping, finance ledger records, configurable annual term order, reviewed legacy liability transfers, student-level physical checklist events, and staff-only registrar follow-up ledgers. Prior enrollments are grouped without changing their IDs, semester labels, subject/schedule links, grades, or old transaction balances. Migrated legacy rows are read-only in the new annual mutation routes. Finance reports distinguish valid collections by payment date, correction records by reversal date, and allocations by charge context; separate subtotals are not combined into grand payer totals. The missing-grade overview separates distinct learners from grading-period entries and does not infer deadlines or historical expected periods.
