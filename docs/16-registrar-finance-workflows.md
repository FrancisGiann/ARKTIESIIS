# Registrar and Finance Workflows

## Interface terminology

- **Pre-enrollment records** are front-desk transcriptions of a student's paper form. A recorded receipt does not mean staff inspected or verified a document.
- **Enrollments** are registrar-managed school-year records with term placements. They keep a link to their required paper pre-enrollment source.
- **Student records** hold the learner's profile and academic history. **Student accounts** in Finance are year-specific fee records; **User accounts** are sign-in access managed by database administrators.
- Teachers keep **My classes** and open each class's **Student list**. Student pages use **Class schedule** and **Fees & payments**.
- Database administrators use **User accounts** for sign-in access, **Student logins** for student sign-in accounts, and **Activity & documents** for the activity log and document workspace. The Activity log names the **Performed by** person or system for each recorded action; stored event names remain unchanged.
- **Documents** are uploaded files. **Paper document records** track received or inspected paper copies separately; receipt, Paper clearance, Finance term account clearance, and document request fee review are distinct records.

## Academic schedules

At `/registrar/schedules`, registrars can check a complete class time while adding or editing it. The warning identifies the matching class, section, teacher, room, day, time, and whether the overlap shares a section, teacher, or room; room availability is checked only when a room is entered. Checks include other sections in the selected term even when the weekly view is filtered. Saving repeats the same check inside its transaction, so a schedule added after the preview is still rejected and the entered values and conflict details remain available for correction.

## Student record navigation

The registrar's record list opens with search and results. Terms and sections are grouped under the records setup view. Opening a learner keeps their identity visible while staff move between Overview, Profile, Academics, Documents, and History. Overview is limited to current status; profile revisions and the existing document-request ledger have their own views. Profile account and enrollment controls are secondary to the learner's details.

The Registrar workspace keeps the selected school-year and term enrollment overview at the top, followed by student search and saved registrar review queues. Review counts cover all school years and have direct matching filters for ready front-desk paper intakes, annual records awaiting entry confirmation, latest actionable digital submissions, and teacher grade submissions. A queue read failure is shown as unavailable without hiding student search; access refusals remain errors. Setup pages stay in secondary navigation. Student overview actions use linked annual-record and term context, and Profile links to annual management while restricting correction controls to existing source-less historical term rows.

Navigation group headings across roles use burgundy text on desktop and mobile; link selection keeps the existing highlight style.

The student Documents workspace uses one shared tab bar for Digital submissions, Paper requirements, staff-only Form 137, and the existing Requests ledger. Requests keeps that bar visible and selects Requests while the parent Documents destination remains current. Staff can open a section from its link or from a legacy paper/Form 137 anchor. With JavaScript enabled, one section is shown at a time; without it, every section remains available. Student views do not expose the staff-only tabs.

## Paper pre-enrollment

Front desk staff and registrars share `/pre-enrollments` to transcribe the paper pre-enrollment form before a student exists in the master list. Entries retain first, middle, last, and suffix names; LRN; student contact number; optional voucher type as written (blank, PUB, ESC, or NV); preferred track and cluster; target grade; last grade and school attended; signature-present and student-signed date; and the paper received-by/date. Voucher category is no longer collected or displayed. Existing category values and revision history stay stored during unrelated corrections. An arbitrary historical voucher string can be kept only by leaving the saved value unchanged; new text must use a canonical choice. The authenticated account is separately recorded as the person who entered or corrected the database record. School year defaults to `2027-2028` and is editable independently of academic-term setup. A draft can be saved while paper details are missing, while every supplied value remains validated, but a save requires at least one applicant detail or an explicitly received paper item. School-year/status defaults, office receipt metadata, and signature-only flags do not count as applicant content. A returning applicant can wait in Draft for an accepted evaluation; an evaluation already supplied is checked for current acceptance and LRN/year/grade/student binding. A Draft never creates a student or annual record. Separate **Save draft** and **Submit as Ready** actions keep completion requirements on Ready submissions. Linked field errors preserve values and focus the error summary. A Ready correction defaults keyboard submission to Ready and keeps an explicit Save draft action; an update without an action retains its saved status. “Ready for registrar” requires the paper fields needed for review, including a complete 12-digit LRN and accepted evaluation for a returning applicant. Duplicate prevention applies to complete LRN plus school year; drafts with blank or partial LRNs can coexist. Email stays visible in the contact section; optional profile, address, family, emergency-contact, and receipt fields use separate native disclosures. A validation error in either optional group opens that group while preserving its submitted values.

A supplied birth date must be a real date before the current day in Asia/Manila; this applies to incomplete front-desk drafts as well as ready records. Blank remains valid. The form's date limit is a convenience only; the server enforces the same Manila-day rule. Nonblank mother, father, and emergency-contact person names must pass the student person-name rule, while Facebook display names and emergency relationship text must contain at least one Unicode letter. These optional fields remain blankable, and repeated household phone numbers and names are allowed. Nonblank structured City and Province values must contain a Unicode letter. Block/lot, street, barangay, and ZIP components may be numeric, including a number-only barangay or street; legacy free-text addresses keep their existing format validation.

Each pre-enrollment record stores original and photocopy receipt indicators and piece counts for eight paper requirements: Report Card, Birth Certificate, Good Moral, Junior High School Certificate, Certificate of Rating (ALS), ESC Certificate (Private School), optional National ID, and three 2×2 pictures. Three long brown envelopes use a separate optional checkbox; checking it means exactly three originals, while clearing it records not received. This checkbox is informational and never blocks readiness or enrollment. Existing partial or unknown envelope history is shown accurately and preserved until staff explicitly changes that control; unrelated corrections do not convert it to three or erase it. All receipt notes are not verification and do not create events in the separate physical-checklist workflow. There is no scan upload, extraction, printout, or spreadsheet import in pre-enrollment. Existing paper/physical review remains a staff decision.

Paper programs use one shared catalog when staff creates new sections. **Academic Track** offers **ASSH (Arts, Social Science, and Humanities)**, **BE (Business & Entrepreneurship)**, and **BE-TECH-PRO HM (Hospitality Management)**. **Tech-Pro Track** offers **Hospitality and Tourism** and **ICT Support & Computer Programming**. New sections store the track in `sections.cluster` and its exact catalog cluster in `sections.strand`; existing section rows and historical placements remain unchanged. Annual intake lists existing sections for the selected school year, grade, and term, including older section labels. A paper record's preferred track and cluster remain visible for registrar review but do not restrict placement. Saving an intake references the selected section row by ID and does not rewrite its metadata or the saved paper preference. Future terms may remain unassigned; in same-section mode, automatic matching still requires an exact section name, track, and cluster match. No legacy program equivalencies are inferred.

Only front-desk staff can create paper pre-enrollment records. Front desk can find, view, and correct entries before conversion starts; the registrar can find, review, and correct a Ready entry and start annual enrollment; database administrators can read the record and its history. Other roles have no access. Draft saves can be incomplete; Ready requires a valid contact email and complete paper-required information. Each change is attributed to the authenticated staff actor and protected by an optimistic revision. A paper record becomes read-only once annual enrollment starts. That source event means a linked annual record was created; it does not prove the registrar confirmed enrollment. The detail page reads the linked annual `intake_status` for its current confirmation state and gives only registrars a direct review or management link.

The registrar opens the guided wizard from a Ready source at `/registrar/intake/new?preEnrollmentId=…`; unsourced new annual-intake access and posts are rejected. The source profile is authoritative. The wizard shows the saved profile and applicant history, uses the matching existing student when present, and requires explicit per-field approval before applying any returning-profile corrections. Student and emergency addresses are each one approval containing the formatted value and every structured component; an unrelated profile edit preserves legacy free-text address values. The registrar still chooses actual PUB/ESC/NV enrollment classification, term, section, and enrollment date. Paper preferences and receipt counts remain visible for review but do not create physical-checklist events. A first student login requires a saved surname and valid past birth date. Missing or invalid details can be corrected in the paper source or, for a matched student, through an explicitly approved profile correction; the transaction checks the authoritative saved values again. Saving the paper source alone creates no master student, login, student number, annual enrollment, term placement, assessment, or finance entry. Conversion links the source and annual record in one transaction; identical source/detail retries return the same record, and stale source, evaluation, profile, or retry details conflict.

Every newly created annual enrollment, including continuing students and readmissions, requires a current Ready front-desk source and immutable front-desk creation provenance. This requirement does not block source-less historical annual records from existing later-term workflows. A readmission additionally requires an accepted registrar evaluation bound to the applicant LRN, school year, target grade, and current evaluation revision; first annual confirmation revalidates the binding. Email changes for linked returning accounts are registrar-approved, uniqueness-checked against active and pending emails, token-invalidating, audited against the user record, and committed with the annual source transaction.

Student and emergency-contact addresses use five visible components: combined Block and Lot, Street/Purok; Barangay; City; Province; and optional ZIP code. ZIP accepts blank or exactly four digits and preserves leading zeroes. Legacy free-text address values remain intact and are shown until staff explicitly selects replacement and transcribes components; unrelated profile edits preserve the stored legacy value. Structured values also retain the existing formatted-address fields for compatible displays and prints.

When converting a paper pre-enrollment, structured address components are validated before composing the compatible formatted address. Thus a valid number-only street/barangay/ZIP is not rejected just because the composite itself has no letters; nonblank city and province are validated from their raw component values. If no structured components were saved, the existing free-text address validation remains in force.

## Annual enrollment

The registrar starts **Evaluate return** from Student records after searching by name or LRN. For a saved student, the action appears only for a latest recorded dropped/transferred departure, or confirmed prior participation with at least one full school-year gap before the configured current year. The effective term anchors a recorded departure, which takes precedence over that annual record's pending or enrolled intake flag. A same-term or later active/pending placement, a newer unresolved intake, previous-year continuity, or an older departure followed by later participation suppresses new review. The target school year does not change eligibility. A missing current-year setup cannot establish a gap; profile status, payment state, missing grades, and missing history alone do not establish an interruption. Existing evaluation statuses and revisions remain readable in the student record after return.

The linked evaluation shows current saved academic context separately from its saved prior-progress findings: the latest enrollment that can be supported by configured year/term order, each recorded enrollment's year/term/grade/section/status, and its subject codes/names with recorded grading-period marks and remarks. Zero is displayed as a grade; missing grades remain missing. These records do not establish subject completion or write grades. A recorded departure's effective term/date is shown separately, and an enrollment label does not claim when or why the student stopped. The full Academics view remains linked. A new return evaluation can only start from an eligible saved student record; if a name or LRN search finds no record, resolve the saved record before beginning the review. Existing unlinked evaluation history remains searchable and reviewable. Acceptance still requires prior progress and evidence reviewed, a curriculum comparison explicitly marked Resolved, required subjects identified, and availability confirmed. The system does not infer equivalencies or make an admissions decision. Form 137 may support the review but is not a new required verified document. Front desk sees only the accepted applicant identity/year/grade/revision projection needed to transcribe a fresh paper record; curriculum, evidence, and decision notes remain restricted to the registrar/admin review. Older `/registrar/readmissions` bookmarks redirect to Student records. See [front-desk intake, return evaluation, and demo reset](19-front-desk-readmission-and-demo-reset.md) for decision, reset, and reseed details.

Registrar intake reviews the student profile, academic placement, school documents, and paper checklist. The registrar chooses PUB, ESC, or NV from the school documents, then saves a recoverable pending intake and may print its private pending school e-form. Saving does not post fees or confirm enrollment. The pending intake keeps `intake_status` as `pending`; a first-time login remains inactive until the final Registrar confirmation. The pending e-form labels Registrar, Finance, and Student copies, shows only saved identity, placement, subjects, and matched schedules, and leaves review/signature, enrolled-stamp, and school-item receipt fields blank.

Finance owns annual fee assessment for new, continuing, transferee, and readmitted intakes. Finance reviews the exact saved annual record in the Finance workspace, selects the matching approved schedule and optional fees, posts the assessment and charges, and records a durable Finance review tied to that assessment, schedule version, voucher, selections, fee composition, and adjustment dependencies. The annual account remains pending. A payment may settle the scheduled Tuition DP installment(s) for that annual record's exact entry term using eligible allocations or supported legacy reconciliations. The remaining annual balance need not be paid before confirmation; unallocated credit, other terms, other years, or unrelated charges do not count toward this DP check. A legitimately zero/net-waived DP requires explicit Finance approval after an active matching schedule, assessment, and current ledger have been reviewed. Missing DP setup or missing fee review is not a zero assessment. The Registrar review displays only whether Finance assessment/review and DP readiness are complete; it does not expose amounts, select fee lines, or create the first annual assessment. Final confirmation rechecks the exact review and live entry-term ledger under transaction locks together with source, placement, paper-clearance, and readmission requirements, then records Registrar confirmation and activates the entry term atomically. Finance changes committed before confirmation can make the review stale or DP unpaid and block finalization. Later financial corrections do not automatically unenroll a confirmed student. A returning learner keeps the existing profile and login; no credential is reset on retries or later-term activation.

**Local UI candidate; not live.** The Registrar's paper-requirements step can place configured original, photocopy, or piece counts into blank count fields when staff first checks a received-and-inspected paper. Staff may edit the counts, including setting them to zero. Unchecking a paper disables its count fields but retains the entries; checking it again does not replace them. Recovered form values are preserved, including zero. Without JavaScript, the count fields remain manually editable and start blank. Counts do not verify or complete a requirement; only the staff checklist decision records that state.

**Local UI candidate; not live.** Finance account-list actions preserve the selected year, term, filters, and return path. Unassessed accounts and accounts needing Finance review link to their assessment or review section. **Record payment** is offered for an assessed account, including pending intakes that still need entry-term DP readiness; the existing server checks remain authoritative for payment eligibility. In the account overview, the primary next action follows the selected year's assessment and review state. This is navigation and page guidance only; assessment posting, Finance review, and Registrar confirmation continue to use their existing server checks.

### Paper term clearance

The dedicated **Paper clearance** workspace has a searchable student-term list and counts scoped by configured school year and term; counts describe term records, not unique students, and status links narrow the list without changing status counts. A student record keeps a compact clearance summary and a link to the focused page. If a read detects the specific missing `recording_mode` column, the student overview still renders with an explicit unavailable summary and infers no status; the workspace and prerequisite reviews explain that clearance data cannot be read. Other database failures remain service errors. Final confirmation and term activation continue to fail closed until prerequisite status can be read; no compatibility fallback marks a term complete. Applicable terms show only **Not completed** or **Completed**. **Not completed** means no completion is recorded; it does not describe whether signatures are being collected. Unreviewed and unfinished legacy records remain Not completed. The registrar records completion with one **Paper clearance completed** checkbox and Save. The server records the authenticated operator, Manila inspection date, version, and audit history automatically. **Term not attended** remains a separately reasoned scope exclusion and is never counted as complete. A registrar can correct applicability through the separate, collapsed **Record term not attended** control for an existing past or current term placement; future and before-entry placements are excluded. This optional correction is not part of the routine completion form.

The checkbox is the registrar's attestation that the paper form has been inspected and meets the completion requirement. The system does not collect or imply per-signature progress. Saving an incomplete term as completed does not require template rows or teacher assignments. Reopening an existing completion requires a correction reason; recording or changing a term-not-attended decision requires its reason, and changing a saved exclusion back to completed requires a correction reason. The exclusion action preserves old template/signature rows and history as evidence, clears only the active completion attestation when applicable, and appends a reasoned audit event. An exact idempotent retry remains valid across days and is bound to the authenticated recorder. Completion and applicability corrections can affect future-term eligibility but do not change placements, Finance, or grades.

Migration `v2.017` template and signature rows remain preserved as historical evidence. The active UI and public write methods no longer create or edit templates or signature lines. Legacy completion still uses its original strict signature-checklist predicate; the application does not infer a new whole-paper completion from old checklist rows. New or explicitly corrected records use the forward-only `v2.018` paper-confirmation mode and keep any prior template, signature, and history data untouched.

For a continuing student's first annual confirmation, the registrar reviews and saves the exact prior-year annual record as the continuity source. Pending v2.016 annual intakes have no source binding after migration; the registrar must make this audited selection before confirmation. The saved source cannot be silently changed and must be the same student's exact preceding school year without a departure. The review includes required attended terms from that source year. Registrar decisions to mark a term not attended require a recorded reason; future terms and placements before the student's saved entry term are excluded by configured year/term order. A continuing Grade 11 learner's Grade 12 choice remains a manual academic review. New students and accepted readmissions are exempt from a prior internal paper-clearance gate for their first annual confirmation; readmission still requires its accepted evaluation. After admission, a later-term activation requires complete paper clearance for each required earlier attended term in that annual enrollment.

Front-desk intake and pending annual enrollment may be prepared while a prerequisite is unresolved. Final annual confirmation and later-term activation stop until the review is complete. A future placement can be prepared but cannot be activated before its configured term becomes current. These checks require one unambiguous current academic term and a complete three-term order for the relevant school year; resolve missing or conflicting term setup through the registrar's term configuration before retrying. Exact completed confirmation/activation retries return their saved result; changed review details require a fresh submission. Reopening paper clearance blocks later confirmations but does not undo an annual enrollment that was already confirmed.

Paper clearance is separate from Finance's term account clearance, payments, balances, arrangements, document checklist, and document request fee review. None of those workflows can mark paper clearance complete. Apply forward-only migration `v2.018` after `v2.017`; it preserves legacy rows, adds an explicit recording mode, and does not backfill historical terms as complete. Term order/current period and any `v2.016` continuing-source bindings still require their existing review; unresolved records fail closed until reviewed.

The initial confirmation response is a private, no-store page that includes the one-time temporary password in a compact footer so the registrar can print or save that response for a private handoff. For an eligible first account activation, the password is the saved surname exactly as stored, followed by the saved birth date as `YYYYMMDD`; suffix is excluded. The surname's case, spaces, hyphens, apostrophes, and Unicode are preserved. A missing/invalid past date or a result over bcrypt's 72 UTF-8-byte limit blocks activation without truncation. Exact values shorter than 12 bytes are valid temporary credentials; the 12-byte minimum applies to permanent password changes/resets. The password is not emailed or stored in plaintext. The student must complete email verification and change it at sign-in. Saved or reopened confirmation copies omit the password; if the initial response is lost, use the existing password reset flow. Returning active accounts and later-term activations never receive a reset password. The saved confirmation includes enrollment identity, current term placements/status, confirmation reference/date, and the saved installment and itemized assessment, including approved coverage. It does not show payment history or represent a receipt. The fee details are reconstructed only from rows and exemption applications at or before confirmation and must match the stored confirmation fingerprint; later supplementary charges and later coverage are excluded, and unverifiable historical details are not replaced with newer schedule rates.

New student enrollment and profile forms validate names as alphabetic text with Unicode letters and marks, spaces, hyphens, apostrophes, and initials; they reject numeric-only names. A supplied birth date must be a real calendar date before today in the Asia/Manila timezone. Optional phone numbers require 7–15 digits and may use a leading plus, spaces, hyphens, and balanced parentheses. An entered address must contain letters and may include street numbers, punctuation, and line breaks. New enrollment records use the fixed gender choices Male, Female, Other, or blank for “Not recorded”; existing profiles retain the exact legacy `unspecified` value until staff changes it. These checks validate entry format, not a learner's identity or the truth of profile details.

If saving an enrollment hits an unexpected server error, the response includes a support reference and returns submitted values with the original idempotency token when term and section options are available. Retrying that submission is safe if the database commit outcome was unclear. If the form cannot be reloaded, check the enrollments list before starting a new submission. Server logs record the reference, operation, sanitized error name/code/number/SQL state, and a repository-relative source location; they do not record the error message, SQL text, stack, submitted values, or student details.

Each annual parent has Grade 11 or Grade 12 and three term placements in explicitly configured order. The registrar chooses one annual section by default; the system maps other term placements only when exactly one section matches the same school year, grade, name, cluster, and strand. When there is no unique match, the registrar can choose a different section or leave a future placement unassigned. No placement may be activated until it has a valid section. A transferee enters in the selected term; earlier terms are not applicable and receive no assessment charges or academic roster entries. A section carries cluster, strand, adviser, modality, and optional modular subtype. Existing enrollment rows keep their identifiers, semester labels, grades, section/subject/schedule links, and history. Migrations `v2.005` and `v2.006` preserve old semester labels and only use explicitly configured annual term mappings; they do not guess which old semester label is Term 1, 2, or 3.

The registrar's guided intake keeps optional family/background fields out of the initial form. Staff can later edit birthplace, Facebook name, emergency contact details, and mother/father names and phone numbers in the student record. These are restricted to registrar and database administrators; finance screens and student views do not receive them. Each changed field is written to the existing append-only profile revision history. Annual administration has a separate focused section for E-form, ESC ID, LIS/VMS, acquaintance waiver/party, educational-tour participation, agreement remarks, and dates items were claimed. These are administrative statuses and dates only; they do not create a charge or infer enrollment eligibility. Annual changes keep actor/date/before/after history. Returning students reach both sets of fields from their existing record.

The registrar's shared roster shows year, grade, voucher, section, cluster, strand, adviser, modality, enrollment date, entry type, gender, placement state, registrar confirmation state, and Finance term account clearance state. The enrollment-count table groups distinct learners by year, grade, configured term, section, cluster, strand, gender, and placement status; it excludes pre-entry not-applicable placements and retired legacy-semester rows. Registrar student records may show only the safe document-request fee summary and request-specific “arrangement exists” indicator. Detailed balances, statements, payments, allocations, arrangement text, and finance notes remain restricted to finance and database administrators.

The active annual intake records one voucher type: PUB, ESC, or NV. The Registrar chooses it from the school documents before saving the pending intake. Older annual records may retain an A–E category value in history, but it is no longer collected or displayed. A Registrar voucher correction after an assessment preserves the original schedule/version and voucher snapshot and invalidates the Finance handoff until Finance re-reviews the current assessment. The assessment is not silently regenerated. Finance records a reasoned resolution and adds separately audited charge adjustments if the reviewed amount changes.

Annual Registrar confirmation, the entry-term tuition DP handoff, and Finance term account clearance are separate records. The Registrar confirms only after Finance has reviewed the assessment and the full scheduled entry-term DP is settled, or Finance has explicitly approved a legitimate zero/net-waived DP. This does not require paying the full annual balance. Finance later records term account clearance independently. Required prior-term paper clearance still blocks confirmation or later-term activation until applicable paper records are complete and the Registrar confirms inspection; the physical document checklist does not replace that paper clearance. Historical term-finance approval rows remain unchanged. Finance roster and student views show Registrar confirmation separately from Finance term account clearance. Finance can record clearance for enrolled, pending, cancelled, dropped, or transferred placements. If a balance remains, the decision requires an arrangement and reason. Clearance never erases debt or automatically cancels finance entries.

**Local finance-restatement candidate; private rehearsal only, not live-authorized.** Migration `v2.024` and its rehearsal-only maintenance utility preserve an immutable before-image for a fixed cohort and add distinct synthetic tuition installment rows from explicitly configured target schedule lines. The original Registrar confirmation, assessment snapshot, and source charge purpose/date remain unchanged. While applied, the effective tuition composition is explicitly labeled as synthetic target-schedule installments and non-tuition fees retain their original schedule provenance; after a recorded logical reversal, the original tuition composition is effective again and synthetic rows remain as inactive history. Payment and allocation history retains recorded placements, releases, and reversals without including inactive rows in current totals. The distinct-ID model has passed a private isolated apply/reversal and typed-preservation rehearsal. A separate committed adjustment changed fresh allocation/release evidence without changing the pinned manifest; the candidate refused apply before writing restatement state and preserved that committed adjustment. Actual concurrent payment/adjustment overlap and a directly observed maintenance-first base-charge lock wait remain unverified. Migration `v2.024` requires the separately deployed `v2.023` companion. The private CLI is not a live executor; do not run this candidate against the live database.

New student logins are activated on the first finalized placement only when the intake still carries first-activation authorization. A subsequent term finalization never resets a password, reactivates a disabled returning login, or invalidates an existing session. Disabling a pending account revokes first-activation authorization. Historical rows do not infer activation eligibility from an inactive account or old pending state. Retired legacy intake/finalization URLs are guarded compatibility paths and do not provide a usable activation workflow.

## Finance schedule and account

Finance configures the shared student-payable amounts and voucher rules for each schedule line by school year, grade, voucher, term, fee category, installment, exact PHP amount, and optional-selection flag. Each new schedule version requires a staff-entered tuition amount for DP, Prelim, Midterm, and Finals in all three terms. Enter `0.00` when an installment has no payable tuition; the application never fills amounts from a voucher, workbook, or another fee line. The fee preview shows configured tuition installments by term when available, distinguishes a configured zero from a missing line, and starts at the learner's entry term. It displays tuition total, other payable fees, and term payable separately so installment columns are not mistaken for the entire term charge. A breakdown is complete only when each applicable term has exactly one nonoptional Tuition row for all four canonical installments; generic, missing, duplicate, mixed, or optional tuition rows remain visible as approved tuition-by-term totals without estimating a split. Saved assessments use their original fee details and never derive a new plan from the latest schedule. Other generic fee lines remain available. Rates are not seeded or hard-coded in school configurations. Schedule changes create another version and never rewrite an existing version or posted assessment snapshot. During intake, the selected voucher loads the applicable active approved schedule in the Finance workspace. Finance reviews that exact schedule version, voucher, optional selections, and approved coverage, then posts charges and durable assessment-review evidence; optional charges are present only when selected. Discounts or exemptions must already be approved and are recorded in the reviewed snapshot.

**Local UI candidate; not live.** **Create fee schedule** and **Revise schedule** open a focused schedule editor; with JavaScript disabled, the editor remains in the page and those links jump to it. Closing the enhanced dialog preserves entered values for reopening. The voucher-tab count is explicitly labeled as saved versions across all years and grades. A payment's **Suggest oldest balances** action is positioned above the manual allocation rows and replaces their current values using the existing suggestion behavior; staff must review the suggested rows before continuing. Adding a fee or balance stays beside the manual editor, and leaving the full amount as unused credit is presented as a separate alternative. The payment review and explicit save remain required. These controls do not alter schedule rates, assessments, payment allocation rules, or stored financial history.

**Local UI candidate; not live.** The enrollment-confirmation print page is an assessment record, not a payment receipt. Its compact print layout retains each saved fee identity and schedule label, gross amount, approved coverage, payable amount, and totals, while making the assessed-not-paid distinction explicit. The print changes affect presentation only; they do not recalculate assessments or change snapshots.

Finance accounts, the student's own Fees & payments page, and the printable Statement of Account share a fee breakdown grouped by school year and term. Each fee shows assessed fee, discounts or exemptions, adjustments, payments applied, balance, and its unpaid, partially paid, fully paid, no-payment-required, or review status. An approved waiver or exemption reduces the assessed fee; voucher rates are already reflected in the schedule and are not deducted again. Signed credit balances are labeled as credits. Legacy whole-term or incomplete tuition rows remain at their saved amounts and labels with an **Installment breakdown not recorded** notice; no estimated installment split or current schedule rate replaces posted history. Staff can expand per-fee payment applications to see the original, still-applied, released, and reversed amounts, available payment dates, and receipt references; legacy reconciliations are labeled as earlier payments, and unavailable dates or references are not reconstructed. Adjustment reasons and fee comments remain Finance-only. Miscellaneous schedule lines require an explicit installment label, which is saved with the schedule and assessment snapshot.

The ledger has separate assessed charge, adjustment, payment, allocation, reversal, and legacy-reconciliation records. Amounts are validated to cents and writes run transactionally with ownership checks and idempotency keys. For each assessed fee, its basis is the assessed amount plus every saved adjustment, including coverage once; a new adjustment cannot reduce that basis below zero. A reviewed reversal of a positive adjustment follows the same cap. Staff can reverse negative historical adjustments one at a time when each correction strictly improves a grandfathered negative basis; the account shows a warning until it is repaired. Statements retain signed remaining due throughout and never clamp an overpayment or negative historical balance. When an accepted correction leaves a nonnegative fee basis below its applied amount, the same transaction releases excess annual payment applications first and eligible legacy reconciliations second as account credit. Transferred-opening-liability guards still apply. Preview displays the before/after fee basis, signed due, and planned releases, then commit rechecks them under lock. Per-fee adjustment history shows every original and reversal; each unreversed original can be submitted once through the reason-required reviewed reversal action. A named supplementary charge supports later retakes, activities, and other approved fees. Corrections append reversals or adjustments; original records remain. A payment amount is recorded once even when allocations span charges, terms, years, or confirmed previous balances. Unapplied payment remains available credit. Finance reviews and confirms a legacy opening liability against the remaining unattributed account balance; the transfer offsets the old account bucket by the same amount so combined debt is unchanged. Existing payment/reconciliation allocations must be explicitly released before transfer, and active transferred amounts cannot be reconciled again. Allocation releases append correction records and restore funds to the same source payment or legacy credit.

For a Registrar-recorded stop or transfer, Finance reviews the saved effective placement and explicitly selects the last tuition installment payable in that departure term, including **None payable**. The preview proposes reductions only for later canonical tuition installments in that term and canonical tuition in later affected terms. Proposals use the current assessed amount plus all saved adjustments, including approved exemptions exactly once; voucher schedule rates are not deducted again. Finance can edit a proposal but cannot reduce a fee below its positive payable basis. Each charge, including retained charges, receives a keep/reduce decision and is saved with its original proposal, reviewed amount, before/after payable basis, and reason. The required overall reason is also used as the per-charge reason when a specific note is blank. Whole-term or noncanonical tuition has no estimated installment split; any reduction must be manually entered. Reduction entries reuse audited charge adjustments and release excess existing allocations to available account credit in the same transaction, while signed balances remain visible. The Registrar's departure event alone never erases debt.

## Registrar follow-up records

The staff-only document-request ledger tracks every requested document type from request through processing, ready, and release, or cancellation. A new request requires finance to record an explicit approval, including a reviewed zero balance; finance can also hold or withdraw an approval. A positive balance approval requires a reason and payment arrangement and leaves the debt unchanged. Registrar users see only whether an arrangement exists and the approving staff member/date. Finance sees the decision reason and private finance note. Students and teachers cannot access these workflows.

Registrar student records show a safe all-years summary: no outstanding balance, with balance, or needs finance review. It includes annual assessments, opening liabilities, and legacy-account balances, but does not treat unallocated annual credit as payment against another charge. An absent account or missing/unverified annual assessment produces “Needs finance review,” never an invented zero. Finance reviews and approves against the exact balance/revision displayed on the finance page. Any supported increase to canonical outstanding debt invalidates that approval; a later decrease does not restore it, and a new increase after a decrease still requires reapproval. Finance decision history is append-only.

After approval, a registrar starts processing and may issue a printable claim slip with a manually selected expected claim date. Issuing a slip does not mark the document ready. The slip contains the school, request reference, student name/number, document, request date, expected claim date, and issuer; it excludes finance details and private notes. Its history is append-only, and rescheduling requires a reason. A slip is printable only while it matches the current finance approval and debt revision. A changed or withdrawn approval requires finance reapproval and a registrar reissue before the slip can be printed or the request can become ready or released. Release still records the actual handover date, recipient, and optional reference; cancellation does not require finance approval.

Request status and finance clearance are separate histories. A request follows requested → processing → ready → released, or can be cancelled before release. Each transition and later correction is append-only with actor and date. Terminal corrections preserve the released or cancelled status and show before/after values with a reason. The workflow does not generate or send a document or grant access to a file.

The staff-only missing-grade overview is based on the exact year, term, section, subject assignment, learner, and grading period. A published grade is authoritative, including a numeric zero; a newer pending workbook revision is shown separately. The overview distinguishes blank published grades, no submission, pending review, correction requested, rejected, and approved-but-unpublished work. Current terms use the configured ECR grading periods. Historical views show observed grading periods only and do not infer missing requirements or deadlines. Distinct learners and learner-period entries are counted separately.

Staff profile edits record changed fields with before/after values, actor, and date in the same transaction as the student update. No-op saves add no revision. Existing profiles receive no invented baseline history; authentication credentials, email, passwords, sessions, and generic log data are excluded. The history is restricted to registrar and database administrator roles.
