# Registrar and Finance Workflows

## Student record navigation

The registrar's record list opens with search and results. Terms and sections are grouped under the records setup view. Opening a learner keeps their identity visible while staff move between Overview, Profile, Academics, Documents, and History. Overview is limited to current status; profile revisions and the existing document-request ledger have their own views. Profile account and enrollment controls are secondary to the learner's details.

Navigation group headings across roles use burgundy text on desktop and mobile; link selection keeps the existing highlight style.

The student Documents workspace separates Digital submissions, Paper requirements, Form 137, and a link to the existing Requests ledger. Staff can open a section from its link or from a legacy paper/Form 137 anchor. With JavaScript enabled, one section is shown at a time; without it, every section remains available. These navigation changes preserve existing route authorization and write workflows.

## Paper pre-enrollment

Front desk staff and registrars share `/pre-enrollments` to transcribe the paper pre-enrollment form before a student exists in the master list. Entries retain first, middle, last, and suffix names; LRN; student contact number; paper voucher type and category as optional transcription text; preferred track and cluster; target grade; last grade and school attended; signature-present and student-signed date; and the paper received-by/date. The authenticated account is separately recorded as the person who entered or corrected the database record. School year defaults to `2027-2028` and is editable independently of academic-term setup. A draft can be saved while paper details are missing. “Ready for registrar” requires the paper fields needed for review, including a complete 12-digit LRN. Duplicate prevention applies to complete LRN plus school year; drafts with blank or partial LRNs can coexist.

Each pre-enrollment record also stores original and photocopy receipt indicators and piece counts for the nine rows on the 2027–2028 paper form: Report Card, Birth Certificate, Good Moral, Junior High School Certificate, Certificate of Rating (ALS), ESC Certificate (Private School), optional National ID, three 2×2 pictures, and three long brown envelopes. These are receipt notes only. They do not mean a document was verified and do not create events in the separate physical-checklist workflow. There is no scan upload, extraction, printout, or spreadsheet import in pre-enrollment. Existing paper/physical review remains a staff decision.

Only front-desk staff can create paper pre-enrollment records. Front desk can find, view, and correct entries before conversion starts; the registrar can find, review, and correct a Ready entry and start annual enrollment; database administrators can read the record and its history. Other roles have no access. Draft saves can be incomplete; Ready requires a valid contact email and complete paper-required information. Each change is attributed to the authenticated staff actor and protected by an optimistic revision. A paper record becomes read-only once annual enrollment starts.

The registrar opens the guided wizard from a Ready source at `/registrar/intake/new?preEnrollmentId=…`; unsourced new annual-intake access and posts are rejected. The source profile is authoritative. The wizard shows the saved profile and applicant history, uses the matching existing student when present, and requires explicit per-field approval before applying any returning-profile corrections. Student and emergency addresses are each one approval containing the formatted value and every structured component; an unrelated profile edit preserves legacy free-text address values. The registrar still chooses actual PUB/ESC/NV enrollment classification, term, section, and enrollment date. Paper preferences and receipt counts remain visible for review but do not create physical-checklist events. Saving the paper source alone creates no master student, login, student number, annual enrollment, term placement, assessment, or finance entry. Conversion links the source and annual record in one transaction; identical source/detail retries return the same record, and stale source, evaluation, profile, or retry details conflict.

Every newly created annual enrollment, including continuing students and readmissions, requires a current Ready front-desk source and immutable front-desk creation provenance. This requirement does not block source-less historical annual records from existing later-term workflows. A readmission additionally requires an accepted registrar evaluation bound to the applicant LRN, school year, target grade, and current evaluation revision; first annual confirmation revalidates the binding. Email changes for linked returning accounts are registrar-approved, uniqueness-checked against active and pending emails, token-invalidating, audited against the user record, and committed with the annual source transaction.

Student and emergency-contact addresses use five visible components: combined Block and Lot, Street/Purok; Barangay; City; Province; and optional ZIP code. ZIP accepts blank or exactly four digits and preserves leading zeroes. Legacy free-text address values remain intact and are shown until staff explicitly selects replacement and transcribes components; unrelated profile edits preserve the stored legacy value. Structured values also retain the existing formatted-address fields for compatible displays and prints.

## Annual enrollment

The registrar-owned `/registrar/readmissions` workflow is reserved for a student returning after an interrupted path. Continuous previous-year participation does not use an evaluation; known departures or gaps require human registrar review. Acceptance requires prior progress and evidence reviewed, a curriculum comparison explicitly marked Resolved, required subjects identified, and availability confirmed. The system does not infer equivalencies or make an admissions decision. Form 137 may support the review but is not a new required verified document. Front desk sees only the accepted applicant identity/year/grade/revision projection needed to transcribe a paper record; curriculum, evidence and decision notes remain restricted to the registrar/admin review. See [front-desk intake, readmission review, and demo reset](19-front-desk-readmission-and-demo-reset.md) for decision, reset, and reseed details.

Registrar intake is a guided five-step flow: student, enrollment, documents received, fees, and a final review of the saved details. “Save and review fees” creates a recoverable pending intake and saves only the selected paper-checklist events in the same transaction; it does not confirm enrollment. On the fee step, the selected PUB, ESC, or NV voucher automatically determines the active approved schedule for the school year and grade. For a complete tuition setup, the page shows Downpayment, Prelim, Midterm, and Finals from the applicable entry term onward, alongside tuition total, other payable fees, and the whole term payable. A split is complete only when each applicable term has exactly one nonoptional Tuition row for each canonical installment. Incomplete or legacy tuition is shown as the approved tuition term total without an estimated split; saved assessments preserve their original fee details. The page also shows selected optional charges and approved coverage. Changing an optional charge requires refreshing the fee total before continuing. The final review reloads student identity, enrollment and term placements, the physical-paper checklist, and the exact fee snapshot from authorized services; it does not trust hidden posted identity values. Staff can return to update profile, placements, checklist, or fee choices. “Confirm enrollment” posts or reuses the versioned assessment shown on that review and activates the entry placement in one transaction. The protected schedule/version, voucher, assessment, selection, snapshot fingerprint, CSRF token, and idempotency key are revalidated on the final POST. Finance maintains shared fee and voucher configuration; the registrar completes the per-student assessment and confirms the whole enrollment without a separate finance-finalization step. Payment collection and recording happens later in finance. A returning learner reuses the linked student profile and login. No credential is reset on retry or later-term activation.

After confirmation, registrars and database administrators can reopen and print the confirmation from the annual record. The print copy includes enrollment identity, current term placements/status, confirmation reference/date, and the saved installment and itemized assessment, including approved coverage. It omits the one-time password and does not show payment history or represent a receipt. The fee details are reconstructed only from rows and exemption applications at or before confirmation and must match the stored confirmation fingerprint; later supplementary charges and later coverage are excluded, and unverifiable historical details are not replaced with newer schedule rates.

New student intake and profile edits validate names as alphabetic text with Unicode letters and marks, spaces, hyphens, apostrophes, and initials; they reject numeric-only names. A supplied birth date must be a real calendar date before today in the Asia/Manila timezone. Optional phone numbers require 7–15 digits and may use a leading plus, spaces, hyphens, and balanced parentheses. An entered address must contain letters and may include street numbers, punctuation, and line breaks. New intake records use the fixed gender choices Male, Female, Other, or blank for “Not recorded”; existing profiles retain the exact legacy `unspecified` value until staff changes it. These checks validate entry format, not a learner's identity or the truth of profile details.

If intake hits an unexpected server error, the response includes a support reference and returns submitted values with the original idempotency token when term and section options are available. Retrying that submission is safe if the database commit outcome was unclear. If the form cannot be reloaded, check the annual intake list before starting a new submission. Server logs record the reference, operation, sanitized error name/code/number/SQL state, and a repository-relative source location; they do not record the error message, SQL text, stack, submitted values, or student details.

Each annual parent has Grade 11 or Grade 12 and three term placements in explicitly configured order. The registrar chooses one annual section by default; the system maps other term placements only when exactly one section matches the same school year, grade, name, cluster, and strand. When there is no unique match, the registrar can choose a different section or leave a future placement unassigned. No placement may be activated until it has a valid section. A transferee enters in the selected term; earlier terms are not applicable and receive no assessment charges or academic roster entries. A section carries cluster, strand, adviser, modality, and optional modular subtype. Existing enrollment rows keep their identifiers, semester labels, grades, section/subject/schedule links, and history. Migrations `v2.005` and `v2.006` preserve old semester labels and only use explicitly configured annual term mappings; they do not guess which old semester label is Term 1, 2, or 3.

The registrar's guided intake keeps optional family/background fields out of the initial form. Staff can later edit birthplace, Facebook name, emergency contact details, and mother/father names and phone numbers in the student record. These are restricted to registrar and database administrators; finance screens and student views do not receive them. Each changed field is written to the existing append-only profile revision history. Annual administration has a separate focused section for E-form, ESC ID, LIS/VMS, acquaintance waiver/party, educational-tour participation, agreement remarks, and dates items were claimed. These are administrative statuses and dates only; they do not create a charge or infer enrollment eligibility. Annual changes keep actor/date/before/after history. Returning students reach both sets of fields from their existing record.

The registrar's shared roster shows year, grade, voucher, section, cluster, strand, adviser, modality, enrollment date, entry type, gender, placement state, registrar confirmation state, and signed clearance state. The enrollment-count table groups distinct learners by year, grade, configured term, section, cluster, strand, gender, and placement status; it excludes pre-entry not-applicable placements and legacy semester rows. Registrar student records may show only the safe document-clearance summary and request-specific “arrangement exists” indicator. Detailed balances, statements, payments, allocations, arrangement text, and finance notes remain restricted to finance and database administrators.

The active annual intake records one voucher type: PUB, ESC, or NV. Older annual records may retain an A–E category value in history, but it is no longer collected or displayed. A registrar may update the voucher type after an assessment. That change preserves the original schedule/version and voucher snapshot and flags an unresolved finance review. The existing assessment is not silently regenerated. Finance records a reasoned resolution and adds separately audited charge adjustments if the reviewed amount changes.

Annual registrar confirmation and signed term-end clearance are separate decisions. The registrar confirms the schedule-based assessment before the entry placement is active; finance later records payments, allocations, and signed clearance. A partial payment, zero-payable assessment, or missing paper checklist does not prevent enrollment confirmation. Historical term-finance approval rows remain unchanged and are not an action in the current annual workflow. Finance roster and student views show registrar confirmation separately from finance's signed term-end clearance. Term-end or departure clearance may be signed for enrolled, pending, cancelled, dropped, or transferred placements. If a balance remains, the signature requires an arrangement and reason. Clearance never erases debt or automatically cancels finance entries.

New student logins are activated on the first finalized placement only when the intake still carries first-activation authorization. A subsequent term finalization never resets a password, reactivates a disabled returning login, or invalidates an existing session. Disabling a pending account revokes first-activation authorization. Legacy rows do not infer activation eligibility from an inactive account or old pending state; staff must verify and explicitly confirm first activation through the reviewed legacy activation page.

## Finance schedule and account

Finance configures the shared student-payable amounts and voucher rules for each schedule line by school year, grade, voucher, term, fee category, installment, exact PHP amount, and optional-selection flag. Each new schedule version requires a staff-entered tuition amount for DP, Prelim, Midterm, and Finals in all three terms. Enter `0.00` when an installment has no payable tuition; the application never fills amounts from a voucher, workbook, or another fee line. The fee preview shows configured tuition installments by term when available, distinguishes a configured zero from a missing line, and starts at the learner's entry term. It displays tuition total, other payable fees, and term payable separately so installment columns are not mistaken for the entire term charge. A breakdown is complete only when each applicable term has exactly one nonoptional Tuition row for all four canonical installments; generic, missing, duplicate, mixed, or optional tuition rows remain visible as approved tuition-by-term totals without estimating a split. Saved assessments use their original fee details and never derive a new plan from the latest schedule. Other generic fee lines remain available. Rates are not seeded or hard-coded in school configurations. Schedule changes create another version and never rewrite an existing version or posted assessment snapshot. During intake, the selected voucher automatically loads the applicable active approved schedule; the registrar reviews and confirms the annual assessment against that exact schedule version, voucher, optional selections, and approved coverage. Confirmation posts term charges from an immutable assessment snapshot; optional charges are present only when selected. Discounts or exemptions must already be approved and are recorded in the reviewed snapshot.

The ledger has separate assessed charge, adjustment, payment, allocation, reversal, and legacy-reconciliation records. Amounts are validated to cents and writes run transactionally with ownership checks and idempotency keys. A named supplementary charge supports later retakes, activities, and other approved fees. Corrections append reversals or adjustments; original records remain. Payment cash is recorded once even when allocations span charges, terms, years, or verified legacy opening liabilities. Unallocated payment remains available credit. Finance reviews and confirms a legacy opening liability against the remaining unattributed account balance; the transfer offsets the old account bucket by the same amount so combined debt is unchanged. Existing payment/reconciliation allocations must be explicitly released before transfer, and active transferred amounts cannot be reconciled again. Allocation releases append correction records and restore funds to the same source payment or legacy credit.

Payment metadata can be appended later when the school receives a transmittal reference, issues a receipt, or needs a private finance remark; recording that metadata does not reverse or duplicate payment cash. Finance can also record an optional handbook number per annual record and append staff-only comments to individual assessed charges. Both keep actor/date history; comments do not modify charges or student statements. Suggested allocations follow oldest outstanding charges for staff review, but staff can edit amounts or add rows before posting. Reports separate valid collections by actual payment date from informational reversal records by correction date; range-wide distinct payer totals are calculated once, not summed from daily or section subtotals. Allocated amounts are reported by target academic context separately from cash collections. Reversals are record corrections and do not claim a cash refund.

Finance navigation is organized by task. The Overview defaults to the configured current school year and term, with whole-term or DP, Prelim, Midterm, and Finals tuition tracking. Its Unpaid, Partially paid, Fully paid, No payment required, and Needs review counts link to the matching paginated roster. Counts and rows use the same eligible placements and authoritative assessed-charge, adjustment, net-allocation, and prior-payment reconciliation balances. A selected installment is shown only when the assessment has an unambiguous canonical tuition split; older whole-term schedules remain usable in whole-term mode and are not given invented installment amounts. Missing assessments or incomplete splits show an unavailable balance and require staff review. Previous account balances and unallocated credit remain separate from term classifications.

Fee schedules are selected by school year and grade, then reviewed in separate PUB, ESC, and NV tabs. The active schedule and term tuition matrix appear first; older versions remain available in a collapsed history. Creating a version follows Context, one-term-at-a-time Fees, Review, and Saved. Existing active amounts prefill only where their actual canonical tuition or other-fee lines can be carried forward; historic whole-term tuition is not copied into other fees or split by estimate. Saving creates a version and leaves existing schedules and assessments intact. Finance controls rates, while registrar intake uses the selected approved voucher schedule and confirms enrollment.

Reports have URL-backed Collections, Allocations, Corrections, and Term balances views. Date filters use Asia/Manila quick ranges or a custom range, capped at 367 days where date filtering applies. Collections count unreversed cash by payment date; allocations describe where existing cash was applied; corrections are dated by the correction event and are not a refund total; term balances are current snapshots and do not change with a date filter. Payer and student links retain the selected report context.

Each report view loads only its own read queries inside a repeatable-read snapshot after a lock-free active finance-role check. Unexpected report failures retain safe database error metadata and a bounded query-phase tag in server logs; the page shows a support reference without exposing SQL, query parameters, or student data.

Annual student accounts have Overview, Payments, Charges & coverage, Clearance & reviews, and History views. Payments use a Details, Allocations, Review, and Saved flow; oldest-due suggestions remain editable, and deliberately unallocated cash stays as credit. A saved payment can be reopened as a guarded printable confirmation that distinguishes original cash from current net allocation and labels the current receipt/reference state. Prior account payments retain guarded confirmation links from annual history. Every Finance mutation, including schedule changes, annual account operations, reviewed prior-payment reconciliation and opening-balance transfers, departure, clearance, and document-clearance decisions, first creates an owner-bound saved review. Each Finance user can keep up to five editable drafts, with at most 64 KiB of normalized input per draft. Draft values survive sign-out and server restart; review approval lasts 20 minutes, and any edit, new sign-in session, or changed dependency requires a fresh review. The final commit rechecks the active actor and current targets under transaction locks, then commits the financial write, audit event, and saved result in one transaction. Staff can resume, edit, re-review, or discard their own pending drafts. The former Legacy account history workspace and its account-creation, transaction-entry, and old enrollment-clearance actions are retired; existing pending drafts for those actions may be discarded by their owner but cannot be resumed, edited, or committed. Their historical rows remain available through annual account history, statements, and guarded payment confirmations. Forward-only migration `012_finance_review_drafts.sql` stores these private drafts. Migration `013_materialize_assessed_charge_due.sql` keeps the shared assessed-charge due calculation on grouped adjustment, net-allocation, and net-reconciliation totals for Finance reports. Migration `014_materialize_legacy_balances.sql` uses separately grouped reconciliation, opening-charge, and payment-allocation totals for the shared legacy-account and opening-liability balances, preserving reversed-payment and release handling without changing ledger rows. The workflow does not issue official receipts.

Finance sees the selected annual balance, all-years annual balance, current-term outstanding amount, prior term/year debt, available credit, and unattributed legacy balance separately. An authenticated staff member or the owning student can open a complete Statement of Account, including history beyond 100 entries, allocations, adjustments, and balances. The statement is printable and does not issue or represent an official receipt. Payment dates are date-only values; receipt/reference and receipt-issued status are recorded independently.

Finance schedules and accounts use private/no-store responses. Student statements show fee names and safe transaction labels but omit private adjustment reasons, legacy descriptions, finance notes, clearance arrangements, and staff-only review details.

Student Finance groups assessed charges by school year and term. Each charge shows its assessed amount, approved coverage, other net adjustments, applied payments, and remaining due; the fee rows and subtotals reconcile with the shared annual ledger. Unapplied payment credit stays separate and is not deducted a second time. The student page and printable statement use safe descriptions and omit private staff reasons, finance notes, and retired legacy-account terminology.

## Physical requirements checklist

The staff-only physical checklist is separate from digital uploads, AI processing, grades, and enrollment blocking. It presents each requirement's latest status and applicability, with quantity fields only for count types enabled by that requirement's definition. Counts are recorded only when known; the configured expected quantity is not copied into the received count. Staff open one item to append a status update and review that item's history. Staff use the existing statuses: pending, received, verified, correction, rejected, and can add named extra requirements per learner with optional quantities. Grade 10 / ALS-AF5 is an alternative JHS card requirement; the Grade 11 card is applicable to Grade 12 learners, not current Grade 11 intake. SF10/Form 137 remains in its own staff-only physical history and appears alongside the checklist summary.

On annual intake, selecting a paper checkbox records the registrar's assertion that the paper was received and checked by staff that day; an unchecked row creates no paper event. The checkbox records a verified status without a separate status selection. Staff can use the student paper-checklist workspace to append other status transitions or correction instructions. Digital uploads and AI prechecks do not create or verify paper-copy events.

ALS and ESC applicability is selected manually by staff; the system does not infer it from PUB, ESC, or NV voucher type. An incomplete checklist is informational and does not block intake or term finalization. Students see only the latest permitted paper-card status/date already exposed by the paper report-card channel; this checklist and its notes stay staff-only.

## Registrar follow-up records

The staff-only document-request ledger tracks every requested document type from request through processing, ready, and release, or cancellation. A new request requires finance to record an explicit approval, including a reviewed zero balance; finance can also hold or withdraw an approval. A positive balance approval requires a reason and payment arrangement and leaves the debt unchanged. Registrar users see only whether an arrangement exists and the approving staff member/date. Finance sees the decision reason and private finance note. Students and teachers cannot access these workflows.

Registrar student records show a safe all-years summary: no outstanding balance, with balance, or needs finance review. It includes annual assessments, opening liabilities, and legacy-account balances, but does not treat unallocated annual credit as payment against another charge. An absent account or missing/unverified annual assessment produces “Needs finance review,” never an invented zero. Finance reviews and approves against the exact balance/revision displayed on the finance page. Any supported increase to canonical outstanding debt invalidates that approval; a later decrease does not restore it, and a new increase after a decrease still requires reapproval. Finance decision history is append-only.

After approval, a registrar starts processing and may issue a printable claim slip with a manually selected expected claim date. Issuing a slip does not mark the document ready. The slip contains the school, request reference, student name/number, document, request date, expected claim date, and issuer; it excludes finance details and private notes. Its history is append-only, and rescheduling requires a reason. A slip is printable only while it matches the current finance approval and debt revision. A changed or withdrawn approval requires finance reapproval and a registrar reissue before the slip can be printed or the request can become ready or released. Release still records the actual handover date, recipient, and optional reference; cancellation does not require finance approval.

Request status and finance clearance are separate histories. A request follows requested → processing → ready → released, or can be cancelled before release. Each transition and later correction is append-only with actor and date. Terminal corrections preserve the released or cancelled status and show before/after values with a reason. The workflow does not generate or send a document or grant access to a file.

The staff-only missing-grade overview is based on the exact year, term, section, subject assignment, learner, and grading period. A published grade is authoritative, including a numeric zero; a newer pending workbook revision is shown separately. The overview distinguishes blank published grades, no submission, pending review, correction requested, rejected, and approved-but-unpublished work. Current terms use the configured ECR grading periods. Historical views show observed grading periods only and do not infer missing requirements or deadlines. Distinct learners and learner-period entries are counted separately.

Staff profile edits record changed fields with before/after values, actor, and date in the same transaction as the student update. No-op saves add no revision. Existing profiles receive no invented baseline history; authentication credentials, email, passwords, sessions, and generic log data are excluded. The history is restricted to registrar and database administrator roles.

## Database and workbook boundary

MariaDB setup uses `database/mariadb/schema.sql` (`v2.001`) and forward-only migrations `002`–`015`; migrations 013–014 materialize grouped Finance balance views, and 015 adds front-desk access, paper pre-enrollment/receipt/revision records, structured address components, and the nullable annual-enrollment source link. Do not edit or rerun an applied baseline or migration. Apply setup only to the explicitly selected existing database, and validate SQL against a disposable MariaDB database. The MariaDB schema does not constrain `documents.supersedes_document_id` from matching that document's own id because MariaDB rejects that auto-increment self-check; `documentService.reupload` rejects self-links before commit and has a service-path test. The sample registrar and finance workbooks are policy context only. No rates or workbook rows are imported, and no import/export feature is included.
