# Demo visual guide

## Mode

Read. The presenter needs to explain verified ARKTIESIIS workflows clearly to a room and move between process diagrams without losing their place.

## Direction contract

Use the established calm school interface: near-white surfaces, readable dark ink, cool rules, restrained school crimson, and a familiar system sans-serif. Give each process one large, projector-readable diagram with clear arrows, numbered actions, actor names, concise outcomes, and correction or exception branches where the flow requires them. Ownership labels carry meaning independently of color; retain the role palette specified in the task. The role overview maps six roles to their own work and links each to related process diagrams without implying shared access to student records.

Keep the first viewport on Enrollment. Provide direct process navigation, role overview, per-step presentation notes, print and download actions, plus keyboard-operable controls and a narrow-screen vertical diagram layout. Preserve source truth and exact screen labels in step details. Keep visual treatment quiet and instructional; use geometry only, no fabricated records, decorative illustration, school seal, or invented policy. Display the full institution name and Lucena Branch clearly.

## Content authority

Base every step on `docs/demo-cheat-sheet.md` and its referenced current implementation. Preserve its distinctions between paper and digital records, student and staff decisions, academic and finance flows, and advisory AI checks versus human acceptance. This brief is development-only and is excluded from downloadable artifacts.
